🎥 The Reel (1 video)

Lead with this — Reels get pushed by the algorithm more than photos. Aim for 15–25 seconds.

Reel 1 — The Casting Call

Concept: ASMR pottery moment → bold call-to-action

Shot list (15–25 sec)

  1. 0–3s · Hook: Close-up of clay being shaped into a tiny mug or matcha bowl. Hands only. Slow, satisfying ASMR-style. This is the scroll-stopper.
  2. 3–6s: Pull back to show the finished mini piece on a wooden board, with the studio in soft blur behind.
  3. 6–10s: Quick cuts of past creators having fun at a class (laughing with friends, painting, glazing). Use existing content if you have it.
  4. 10–14s: One smiling creator holding their finished piece, looking proud.
  5. 14–22s: Final mini piece beauty shot — held in hand, well-lit, against a warm background.

Text overlays (on-screen)

📣 Calling Toronto creators! Get a free class ✨ Post 1 Reel + 1 TikTok 🎥 Apply by June 20 → link in bio

Audio

Pick a trending calm/aesthetic audio on Instagram Reels (search "aesthetic" or "soft trending" in Reels audio library). Avoid loud or chaotic sounds — match the cozy studio vibe.

FAQs (paste as a "Section" with title "Read this first 👇")

Q: Can I bring someone with me?
A: Yes! Bring one other person to help you film — ideally a friend, partner, or date you'd love to feature in your content. We want to celebrate moments shared with people, not just solo shots.
Q: How long does the Reel need to be?
A: At least 60 seconds. No max — go as long as the story is good. We don't need it scripted; just authentic.
Q: When do I post the Reel and TikTok?
A: Within 3 weeks of your class. That's when your finished pottery is ready to take home (after kiln-firing), so your video can show both the making AND the finished piece 🪴
Q: Can I see examples?
A: Yes! Check our Instagram @minipotterystudio — past creator collabs are tagged. The vibe of Clay With Me's creator content is also a great reference: soft, satisfying, very Toronto. [Add specific reel links here before publishing]
Q: Do I have to be a "big" creator to apply?
A: No! We love working with nano and micro creators (1K–50K followers) who have engaged audiences. Authentic > big.
Q: Where is the studio?
A: Downtown Toronto. We'll send the address once you're confirmed.

✅ Quick Launch Checklist
